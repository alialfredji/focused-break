## Focused Break 0.7.0

A native macOS focus timer with visible desktop break reminders.

- Focus sessions from 2 to 120 minutes and configurable break protection.
- Have a break animation, black hole, or your own saved image/GIF.
- Optional live desktop distortion, reduced motion, and a ten-second test.
- Compact focus controls and a clearly grouped Break appearance panel.
- The app returns to the front after a break, ready for your next focus session.

### Install

Requires macOS 14 or later and Metal support. Download **arm64.dmg** for Apple Silicon or **x86_64.dmg** for Intel. Open it and drag Focused Break into Applications. ZIP archives and SHA-256 checksums are also attached.

These builds are **ad-hoc signed, not Apple-notarized**. macOS may require an app-specific **Open Anyway** approval in Privacy & Security. See the [installation guide](https://github.com/alialfredji/focused-break/blob/main/docs/INSTALL.md).

Quit the old version before replacing it. Settings and your saved image remain; active sessions do not survive quitting. Optional screen-recording access may need to be re-enabled after an update.

Both architectures are built and unit-tested in GitHub Actions. Multiple-display, fullscreen, and Spaces behavior still needs broader real-device testing. No automatic in-app updater is included; use the latest-release page for updates.
