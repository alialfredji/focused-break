# Focused Break

**Focus on your work. Take your breaks seriously.**

A Mac focus timer that makes breaks hard to miss. Work with a clear screen, then let a moving image or black hole remind you to step away.

[Download the latest release](https://github.com/alialfredji/focused-break/releases/latest) · [How to use it](docs/USAGE.md) · [Installation](docs/INSTALL.md) · [Privacy](docs/PRIVACY.md)

## What it does

- Choose focus sessions from **2 to 120 minutes**.
- Pick the **Have a break** animation, a **black hole**, or **your own image or GIF**.
- Add desktop distortion, or reduce motion for a calmer break.
- Give yourself **5–300 seconds** before a break can be paused or ended. You can always quit.
- When your break ends, the app comes forward so you can start your next session.

No account or subscription. Your settings and image stay on your Mac.

## Install

Requires **macOS 14 or later** and a Metal-capable Mac.

| Your Mac | Download |
| --- | --- |
| Apple Silicon (M1 or later) | [Apple Silicon DMG](https://github.com/alialfredji/focused-break/releases/latest/download/Focused-Break-macOS-arm64.dmg) |
| Intel | [Intel DMG](https://github.com/alialfredji/focused-break/releases/latest/download/Focused-Break-macOS-x86_64.dmg) |

Open the download, drag **Focused Break** into **Applications**, then open it. To check your Mac's chip, choose Apple menu → **About This Mac**.

**First launch:** the app is not yet notarized by Apple. If macOS blocks it, follow the **Open Anyway** steps in the [installation guide](docs/INSTALL.md).

To update, quit the app and replace it with the latest download. Your settings and saved image remain.

## Your first session

1. Choose **Focus time** and **Break time**. **End break after** sets how long you must wait before ending a break early.
2. In **Break appearance**, choose your visual and size.
3. Try **Preview → Test on desktop · 10s** to see how it looks.
4. Press **Start focus**. When your focus time ends, the break appears.
5. When the break finishes, Focused Break opens in front. Press **Start next focus** when you are ready.

Closing the window keeps the timer running. Use the menu-bar icon to reopen it, pause, or quit.

You can still click through the break effect and use your Mac. [More about appearance, motion, and controls](docs/USAGE.md).

## Screen access

**Distort desktop** and **Live desktop** preview need screen-recording permission. Screen content stays on your Mac and is not saved or uploaded. The basic reminder works without this permission. [Privacy details](docs/PRIVACY.md).

Protected video may appear blank, and effects can vary across multiple displays and fullscreen apps.
